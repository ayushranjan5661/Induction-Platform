# Meet LevelShift: Our Founders and Leadership Team

> **Source:** levelshift.com/leadership and each leader's profile page, read on 26 November 2026. Only facts stated on the website are included here. Three people (Thiyagarajan Velayutham, Rajamannar Abboy and Ramki Ramakrishnen) are listed on the site without a profile page, so we show only their names and titles.

## Our Mission

> "Enable customers to become AI-native enterprises by embedding AI across core business functions."

## Our Vision

> "To be the trusted strategic partner for organizations navigating their transformation journey, delivering tailored, future-ready technology solutions that drive business evolution."

## What Our Leaders Believe

Five beliefs come up again and again in our leaders' own words:

- **People define the value.** *"Technology defines the era, but people define the value."* (Kiran Babu Chandra, CEO)
- **Excellence you can repeat.** *"Scale is earned through repeatable excellence."* (Chandramohan Parlapalli, COO)
- **AI as an enabler, not a replacement.** Understand AI deeply and stay in control of it. (Manoj Chandra, CTO)
- **Discipline and trust.** Keep financial data secure and use it with discipline and trust. (Rangesh Rajaram, CFO)
- **Data comes first.** *"The integrity and authenticity of your data directly determines the quality of your AI outcomes."* (Kumar Vellore)

## Our Leaders

### Kiran Babu Chandra: Chief Executive Officer

- **With the organisation:** Founder
- **Education:** MBA, Business Administration and Management
- **Known as:** Strategic Thinker · People First Leader · Composed Catalyst · Aspiring Film Director

As CEO, Kiran guides LevelShift's strategic direction and growth. His experience spans technology, operations and business leadership, which gives him a balanced view of how to build organizations that adapt and succeed in a fast-changing world. He believes people drive transformation. He invests heavily in developing future leaders, encourages strategic thinking, and helps teams face challenges with confidence. He focuses on lasting impact through people, purpose and continuous learning.

> *"Technology defines the era, but people define the value. Building an AI-native enterprise is a journey of carrying human purpose across the digital divide ensuring that even in times of drastic transition, the human story remains the lead."*

### Chandramohan Parlapalli: Chief Operating Officer

- **With the organisation:** Since 2001
- **Education:** M.Tech, Chemical Engineering
- **Known as:** Adaptive Leader · Process Optimizer · Balanced Thinker · Wine Explorer

Chandramohan turns strategy into execution and makes sure LevelShift runs well at scale. He brings people, processes and technology together so that teams perform at their best and customers succeed. He has spent more than two decades in technology services, working in consulting, delivery leadership, strategic sourcing and business operations with global customers across many countries. He is curious and hands-on. He likes to understand how things work and to improve them steadily, one step at a time.

> *"Scale is earned through repeatable excellence: disciplined processes, data-driven decisions, accountable ownership, and teams confident in both the process and the AI behind it."*

### Manoj Chandra: Chief Technology Officer

- **With the organisation:** Since 2000
- **Education:** B.E., Computer Science
- **Known as:** Detail-Oriented · Tech Enthusiast · Auto Aficionado

Manoj leads technology innovation at LevelShift. He is responsible for delivery strategy, client engagement, technology management and talent development. He has 25+ years in consulting and software development, including work with Microsoft, Intel and IBM FileNET. Over his career he has architected enterprise solutions, managed complex projects and mentored future leaders. He is always curious about what comes next, from how AI works under the surface to the latest software trends.

> *"AI will reshape everything, but the real advantage lies in understanding it deeply, keeping control, and using it as an enabler, not a replacement."*

### Rangesh Rajaram: Chief Financial Officer

- **With the organisation:** Since 2000
- **Education:** B.E., Computer Science
- **Known as:** Fiscal Philosopher · Rajini Admirer · Paws Protector

Rangesh oversees finance, compliance, vendor management and administration, the structure that supports sustainable growth. He has 25+ years of experience across consulting, technology, financial systems and corporate finance, with organizations such as EDS, Capgemini and BearingPoint. He has designed reporting systems for government agencies and applies GAAP to financial reporting, internal controls and compliance. He is passionate about financial literacy and informed decision-making.

> *"The future of finance will be powered by AI, but the real responsibility lies in securing financial data and enabling people to use it with discipline and trust."*

### Muthukumar Ramalingam: Chief Marketing Officer

- **With the organisation:** Since 2001 (his bio says he has been part of the LevelShift story since 2005)
- **Education:** MBA
- **Known as:** Lifelong Learner · Finds Humour in Everything · History Enthusiast

Muthu leads LevelShift's marketing and works with teams across the company to support growth, strengthen customer relationships and bring the company's vision to life. He has worked in software development, delivery management, operations, quality and marketing. That path taught him that the best outcomes come when people, processes and ideas work together. He brings teams together, turns ideas into action and stays optimistic through challenges.

> *"As AI reshapes how customers discover and decide, journeys are no longer linear. Our focus is to stay attuned to customer intent, responding with the right context and timing to keep every interaction relevant and meaningful."*

### Selva Pandian: Managing Director, Cloud Practice

- **With the organisation:** Since 2000
- **Education:** General Management and Leadership
- **Known as:** Lifelong Learner · Basketball Enthusiast · Practical Idealist · Positive Realist

Selva leads the Cloud Practice. He helps organizations use cloud, data, AI and analytics to solve complex business problems. He was one of the founding members of LevelShift's **On Demand Services** model. He has 25+ years in technology, covering enterprise-scale Salesforce implementations, data and analytics platforms, and AI solutions. He believes technology works best when it is designed around people, and he emphasizes empathy alongside innovation.

> *"AI isn't just a shift in technology, it's a shift in mindset. The real advantage lies in taking an AI-first approach, aligning teams early, and capturing the upside of AI through how services are reimagined."*

### Kumar Vellore: Managing Director, Data, Analytics and EI

- **With the organisation:** Since 2023
- **Education:** MBA, Management of Technology, Georgia Institute of Technology
- **Known as:** Tech Futurist · Music Explorer · Coffee Craftsman
- **Recognition:** Top 50 Data & Analytics Professional, 2026 winner (ONCON ICON Awards). Featured by *The Tycoon Times* as a Top Global Leader.

Kumar leads Data, Analytics and Enterprise Intelligence. He helps businesses make smarter decisions, improve customer experiences and create lasting value through modern data and analytics. He has led large-scale transformations across industries, from modernizing data platforms to putting AI-powered systems in place. He believes the future belongs to organizations that combine data, AI and human judgment well.

> *"The future of AI isn't just about building smarter models — it's about getting your data ready for them. The integrity and authenticity of your data directly determines the quality of your AI outcomes."*
