## 1. POSH — Prevention of Sexual Harassment

### What Is POSH?

**POSH** stands for **Prevention of Sexual Harassment at the Workplace**. In India, it is governed by the *Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013*.

Its objective is to give every employee a workplace that is:

- Safe
- Respectful
- Inclusive
- Free from harassment and discrimination

### Employee Responsibilities

Every employee must:

- Treat colleagues with dignity and respect.
- Maintain professional boundaries.
- Avoid inappropriate verbal, physical, or digital behavior.
- Report incidents without fear of retaliation.
- Cooperate during investigations.

### Examples of Unacceptable Conduct

| Type | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Verbal** | Sexually suggestive comments · Inappropriate jokes · Offensive remarks |
| **Physical** | Unwanted touching · Physical intimidation · Invasion of personal space |
| **Digital** | Inappropriate messages · Sharing offensive content · Unwanted communication through email, Teams, WhatsApp, or social media |

### Reporting Mechanism

If you experience or witness harassment:

1. **Report the incident immediately.**
2. **Contact HR or the Internal Committee (IC).**
3. **Provide relevant details and evidence.**
4. **Cooperate with the investigation.**

### LevelShift's Commitment

- Zero tolerance toward harassment
- Fair and confidential investigations
- Protection against retaliation
- Respect for all employees

## 2. Information Security

Information and cybersecurity are **everyone's responsibility** at LevelShift.

### Why Security Matters

LevelShift handles:

- Customer data
- Business information
- Intellectual property
- Employee information
- Project documentation

A security breach can affect customers, employees, and business operations.

### Security Principles (CIA Triad)

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Confidentiality** | Information is accessible only to authorized individuals. |
| **Integrity** | Information remains accurate and unaltered. |
| **Availability** | Systems and data are available when required. |

### Employee Security Responsibilities

#### Account Security
- Use strong passwords.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Never share credentials.
- Lock your system when you step away.

#### Email Security
- Verify sender authenticity.
- Report phishing attempts.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links.
- Open attachments only from trusted sources.

#### Device Security
- Use approved devices.
- Keep systems updated.
- Install authorized software only.
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately.

#### Remote Work Security
- Use secure networks.
- Connect through VPN where required.
- Prevent unauthorized access to company systems.
- Protect confidential information when working remotely.

### Common Security Threats

| Threat | Description |
|---|---|
| **Phishing** | Fraudulent emails that attempt to steal credentials or information |
| **Malware** | Malicious software designed to damage systems or gain unauthorized access |
| **Social Engineering** | Manipulating people into revealing confidential information |
| **Data Leakage** | Accidental or intentional exposure of sensitive information |

## 3. Data Protection

Data protection ensures that customer, employee, and company information is collected, processed, stored, and shared responsibly.

### Types of Protected Data

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| **Customer Data** | Business information · Project data · Contact information · Transaction records |
| **Employee Data** | Personal information · Payroll records · Performance records · Identification documents |
| **Company Data** | Financial information · Strategic plans · Intellectual property · Internal communications |

### Data Protection Principles

| Principle | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Collect Only What Is Needed** | Access and use only the information necessary for business purposes. |
| **Use Data Responsibly** | Use data only for authorized activities. |
| **Protect Sensitive Information** | Apply appropriate security controls and access restrictions. |
| **Share Carefully** | Share information only with authorized individuals and platforms. |
| **Dispose Securely** | Delete or archive data according to company retention policies. |

### Employee Responsibilities

Employees must:

- Follow confidentiality agreements.
- Access data only when authorized.
- Avoid storing company data on personal devices.
- Protect customer information.
- Report data incidents immediately.
- Comply with company and regulatory requirements.

### Best Practices

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Use company-approved applications | Share passwords |
| Classify information appropriately | Forward confidential information without authorization |
| Verify recipients before sending emails | Use unauthorized cloud storage services |
| Report security concerns immediately | Download sensitive data unnecessarily |
| Keep software updated | Leave devices unattended and unlocked |
