## The Living Enterprise and ShiftEVA

LevelShift's big idea is the **Living Enterprise**: *an enterprise that senses, learns and evolves.* The platform behind it is **ShiftEVA**, a transformation platform built in three steps:

1. **Reimagine** for autonomy
2. **Observe** through unified telemetry
3. **Learn** from a feedback loop

*"The result, an enterprise built to evolve."* A Living Enterprise has:

- Lower decision latency
- Less organizational friction
- Increased autonomy
- Continuous learning
- **Humans in control**

## What We Deliver

The home page groups our services under **"Preparing the enterprise for autonomy"**: AI Readiness, Data Modernization, Enterprise Integration, Enterprise Applications, and AI Governance & Security.

### 1. Enterprise AI

Helps customers move AI from pilot to production.

- **Six disciplines:** AI Strategy · AI Architecture · Data Governance for AI · Data and Platform Engineering · AI Development and Intelligence · AI Change Management and Adoption
- **Value Realization Framework, in five phases:** Strategic Clarity → Functional AI Discovery → Quantification → Architecture and Enablement → Controlled Execution
- **AI Center of Excellence:** 150+ pre-built agents (the AI Agents Library) and proven frameworks
- **Ways to start:** a 10-minute AI Maturity Assessment or a one-day Discovery Workshop
- We aim to be the customer's **single accountable partner**

### 2. Data Modernization

- **LevelShift Data Modernization Framework (LDMF)**, built in three layers: Foundation, Transformation and Activation
- **Strategy and Advisory:** the Data360 Opportunity Scan, data strategy, analytics roadmap and platform strategy
- **Cloud Adoption:** native architectures on Azure and AWS, re-platforming and containerization
- **Data Governance and Compliance:** Microsoft Purview, Databricks Unity Catalog, AWS governance and Perforce Delphix, covering GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA and PCI-DSS
- **Data Security:** encryption, masking, role-based access and zero trust
- **Accelerators:** Power BI industry accelerators, Microsoft Fabric accelerators, and a Tableau-to-Power BI migration accelerator
- **Credentials:** Microsoft Solution Partner (100+ certified professionals), Microsoft Fabric Featured Partner (50+ Fabric-certified experts), strategic Databricks partner, 100+ data transformations completed, winner of the Top 10 Data and Analytics Team Awards
- **Leaders:** Kumar Vellore (MD, Data, Analytics and EI) and Jonathan Wilcox (Director, Enterprise Architecture)

### 3. Enterprise Integration

*"Enable AI and agentic enterprise through connected apps, unified data, and orchestrated workflows."*

- **Services:** consulting and architecture, application integration (Salesforce, Microsoft, ERP), data integration and master data management, hybrid cloud, APIs, AI-led and event-driven integration, and managed services backed by SLAs
- **Platforms:** Boomi (Boomi360), MuleSoft, Workato, Azure Integration (Logic Apps, API Management, Event Grid, Service Bus), Jitterbit and Celigo
- **Six-step approach:** Analyze and plan → Design → Build, test and validate → Secure and comply → Deploy → Monitor and optimize
- **Credentials:** an Integration Centre of Excellence with 100+ certifications, 5+ agentic solutions and 1,000+ services integrated. We are platform-agnostic.
- **Leaders:** Kumar Vellore and Dhanasekaran Vasudevan (AVP, Enterprise Integration)

### 4. Enterprise Applications

*"Your Gateway to Digital Transformation"*, covering front, middle and back-office processes.

- **Salesforce:** CRM, Data Cloud and Agentforce AI agents. Strategy, implementation, modernization and on-demand support.
- **Microsoft Business Applications:** Dynamics 365 ERP and CRM. Advisory, migration, custom development and support.
- **SAP:** S/4HANA strategy, implementation and migration, plus SAP BTP, analytics and managed services
- **Credentials:** Salesforce Summit Partner, Microsoft Solutions Partner, a 4.9 rating on AppExchange, and our distinctive **On Demand Service** model

### 5. DevOps and Test Data Management

- **Seven pillars:** TDM strategy and governance · data privatization · synthetic data generation (GenRocket) · data mining · platform and infrastructure support · agentic AI automation · ongoing optimization
- **Technology partners:** Perforce (Delphix, BlazeMeter, Perfecto), GenRocket and antenna.dev
- **Credentials:** 25+ years of experience, 50+ Perforce Delphix-certified specialists, 150+ Microsoft experts and a dedicated TDM Centre of Excellence

### 6. Business Process Services

*"Reinvent Workflows. Accelerate Outcomes."* AI and machine-learning workflows, with people reviewing the results.

- Medical record review (for IMEs, QMEs, life care planners, attorneys and insurers)
- Lease management (CAM reconciliation, lease abstraction, audits)
