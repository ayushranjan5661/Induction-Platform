# Meet LevelShift: Our Founders, Our Leaders, Our Story

## Our Mission

To help organizations turn technology into real business outcomes by bringing together people, processes, and technology across cloud, data, analytics, AI, and enterprise applications. We want solutions that are technically sound and that also make work simpler and better for the people who use them.

## Our Vision

To be a trusted transformation partner for customers around the world, and a place where people grow into leaders. We want to be known for curiosity, getting things done, and lasting impact.

## What We Believe

- **People drive transformation.** Technology succeeds when teams are empowered, mentored, and trusted.
- **Curiosity and continuous improvement.** We keep learning, adapting, and getting better, one improvement at a time.
- **Human-centered innovation.** AI and automation work best when they are designed with empathy.
- **Discipline and integrity.** Sound governance, financial stewardship, and transparency support growth that lasts.
- **Collaboration.** The best outcomes come when people, processes, and ideas work together.

## Our Growth Story

### Built by Founders from Many Industries

LevelShift was founded by entrepreneurs with experience in engineering, aerospace, medical equipment, international finance, supply chain, and manufacturing, in both India and the US. **Rajamannar Abboy** and **Ramki Ramakrishnen** brought a builder's mindset from running businesses across continents. That mindset still shapes how LevelShift plans, runs, and grows.

### Growing with Our People (2005 Onward)

Many of our leaders have grown with the company. **Muthukumar Ramalingam** joined in 2005 and has worked in development, delivery, operations, quality, and marketing. His career shows how LevelShift develops talent from within.

### New Ways to Deliver Value

LevelShift launched its **On Demand Services** model, with **Selva Pandian** as a founding member, to give customers flexible, high-impact access to expert talent. The company's capabilities grew into cloud, Salesforce, data, analytics, and AI.

### Growing Through Partnership

LevelShift brought **Sybrant Technologies**, an IT services firm built by **V. Thiyagarajan (VT)**, into the company, adding VT's three decades of experience in building and scaling businesses.

### Today

LevelShift is guided by a leadership team whose members mostly have 25+ years of experience, gained at organizations such as Microsoft, Intel, IBM FileNET, EDS, Capgemini, and BearingPoint. Our focus is cloud, data, AI, and intelligent automation, delivered for customers around the world.
