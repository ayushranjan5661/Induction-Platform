# LevelShift — Company Overview

## Who We Are

LevelShift is a global AI transformation and technology consulting company founded in 1998. It brings together the strengths of **PreludeSys**, **DemandBlue**, and **DemandDynamics** under a single brand focused on helping enterprises become AI-native organizations.

### Key Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Founded** | 1998 |
| **Headquarters** | Irvine, California, USA |
| **Delivery Model** | Global delivery, serving organizations worldwide |
| **Customers** | 300+ |
| **Team Size** | Approximately 500–1,000 employees |
| **Recognition** | Great Place to Work® certified for multiple consecutive years |

### Vision

To be the trusted strategic partner helping organizations successfully navigate digital and AI transformation while delivering future-ready technology solutions.

### Mission

To enable customers to become AI-native enterprises by embedding artificial intelligence across core business functions and processes.

### What Makes LevelShift Different

LevelShift combines:

- Deep industry expertise
- AI Centers of Excellence
- Salesforce ecosystem knowledge
- Microsoft technology expertise
- Data and integration capabilities
- End-to-end transformation services

The company focuses on delivering **measurable business outcomes** rather than simply implementing technology.

## Our Journey

### 1998 — Where It All Began

**PreludeSys** was founded, focusing on:

- Enterprise systems
- Managed IT services
- Digital operations
- Technology enablement for businesses

This established the foundation for what would later become LevelShift.

### 2012 — Expansion into Salesforce

**DemandBlue** was launched to provide:

- Salesforce consulting
- CRM transformation services
- On-demand Salesforce expertise
- Enterprise-scale Salesforce implementations

DemandBlue grew to become a **Summit-tier Salesforce partner**.

### 2020 — Expansion into the Microsoft Ecosystem

**DemandDynamics** was introduced to focus on:

- Microsoft Dynamics 365
- Microsoft business applications
- Enterprise modernization projects
- Digital transformation services

This expanded the organization's expertise across Microsoft's technology stack.

### 2021–2027 — Workplace Recognition

The company earned repeated **Great Place to Work®** certifications, reflecting its employee-focused culture and organizational excellence.

### 2025 — Birth of LevelShift

PreludeSys, DemandBlue, and DemandDynamics were unified under a single brand: **LevelShift**.

This strategic transformation created:

- One unified, AI-first identity
- Cross-platform expertise
- Stronger AI capabilities
- A single transformation partner for customers
- A focus on AI-native enterprise transformation

### Today

LevelShift serves as an AI transformation partner helping enterprises leverage:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Data modernization
- Enterprise integration
- Cloud technologies
- Salesforce solutions
- Microsoft technologies

— with the goal of building **intelligent, adaptive, and future-ready enterprises**.
