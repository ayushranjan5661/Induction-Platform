# LevelShift Policies

This guide summarizes the key workplace policies every LevelShift employee should know. For complete details, entitlements, and the latest updates, refer to the official HR policy documents or contact HR.

---

## 1. Leave Policy

LevelShift encourages employees to maintain a healthy work-life balance while ensuring business continuity.

### Types of Leave

| Leave Type | Notes |
|---|---|
| Privileged / Earned Leave (PL/EL) | Planned vacations and personal time |
| Casual Leave (CL) | Short, unplanned personal needs |
| Sick Leave (SL) | Illness or medical needs |
| Maternity Leave | As per company policy and applicable law |
| Paternity Leave | As per company policy |
| Bereavement Leave | Loss of a family member |
| Compensatory Off | Where applicable, for approved extra work |
| Public and Company Holidays | As per the published holiday calendar |

### Employee Responsibilities

- Apply for leave through the approved **HRMS system**.
- Obtain **manager approval** before taking planned leave.
- Inform your reporting manager **immediately** in case of emergency or illness.
- Complete a proper **knowledge transfer** before extended leave.
- Stay within your leave balances and follow company leave policies.

### Best Practices

- Plan vacations in advance.
- Avoid taking leave during critical project milestones whenever possible.
- Keep team members informed about your availability and handovers.

## 2. Attendance Policy

Regular attendance demonstrates professionalism, accountability, and commitment to team goals.

### Attendance Expectations

Employees are expected to:

- Adhere to assigned work schedules.
- Join meetings on time.
- Remain available during working hours.
- Keep calendars updated with leave, holidays, and planned absences.
- Follow the organization's hybrid, remote, or in-office attendance guidelines.

### Attendance Standards

| Area | Expectations |
|---|---|
| **Punctuality** | Start work as per team requirements · Attend customer and internal meetings on time · Inform stakeholders about delays whenever possible |
| **Reporting Absences** | Notify your manager promptly · Update attendance systems accurately · Provide supporting documentation if required |
| **Remote Work** | Ensure stable internet connectivity · Maintain productivity standards · Be reachable during agreed business hours |

## 3. Code of Conduct

LevelShift expects all employees to maintain the highest standards of professional and ethical behavior.

### Professional Behaviour

Employees should:

- Treat everyone with respect and dignity.
- Communicate professionally.
- Demonstrate teamwork and collaboration.
- Foster an inclusive workplace.

### Integrity and Ethics

Employees must:

- Act honestly and transparently.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Comply with company policies and legal requirements.

### Workplace Respect — Zero Tolerance

LevelShift has **zero tolerance** for:

- Harassment
- Discrimination
- Bullying
- Retaliation
- Unethical conduct

> If you experience or witness any of these, report it to your manager or HR.

### Information Security

Employees are expected to:

- Safeguard customer and company data.
- Use company resources responsibly.
- Follow cybersecurity policies.
- **Report security incidents immediately.**
